# JB’s class-night guide

**Private rehearsal · Use fictional people, payments and email addresses.**

| When | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Before doors open | On your staff device, check the class date, start, end and late window. The wall QR opens the portal. Leave the shared tablet on check-in. |
| Returning walk-in | Ask for their member reference, such as **AM42**. Check-in opens an hour before class. No booking is needed. If they try twice, check the register before adding anything. After the late window, record the actual arrival in Coach view with a reason. |
| First visit | They can sign up on their phone. If they have no phone, use staff-assisted signup on your device. Let the person read and accept the waiver. Check the record shows who accepted it, the time and version. Ask separately about photos and video. They can decline. |
| Under 18 | Record the guardian’s contact and mark approval pending. Keep attendance and clearance to train separate. Before live use, agree the consent procedure with safeguarding and the insurer. If verbal consent is approved, record the guardian’s name and relationship, call time, exact agreement and your staff identity. Follow the agreed clearance decision before training. |
| Taking payment | For cash, confirm the member, amount and what it covers, then record it once. A transfer uses **AM42** for a top-up or **MEMBERAM42** for membership. Check the current monthly rate (initially £70). After matching, check the next-class start and renewal date with the member. |
| Half an hour after class | Refresh and reconcile the bank feed. For an unclear transfer, compare the original reference, amount, transaction ID and payer name if available. Confirm the member and payment type yourself. Leave uncertain money in review. Only remind a checked-in member who still owes for that class after a fresh reconciliation. |

**If something fails**

- **Internet down.** Keep a private, staff-held paper register of references, arrival times and cash received. Use the club’s agreed offline waiver and clearance process. If clearance cannot be established, pause training for that person. Once online, compare each entry with the register before entering it once, with the outage as the reason.
- **Bank or email down.** Hold reminders while the feed is stale or a possible payment needs review. For manual reconciliation, use the original bank transaction ID so a later import can recognise it. For email failures, retry the existing confirmation job after recovery. Record corrections with a reason.

**Rehearse before opening.** Use a member phone, a shared tablet and a separate staff session. Try assisted signup, pending consent, a late arrival and a double check-in. Match cash and an unclear transfer, correct one entry, then interrupt and restore bank and email service. Compare the member’s balance with Coach view and count confirmations. Ask JB to confirm the class register and outstanding payments.
